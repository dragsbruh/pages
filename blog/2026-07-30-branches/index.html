have i always been this wrong

i used to think that, i would always love computers. for a few years now, i have had fun writing code, building things, working on projects, seeing them work. i think at some point in time, i lost track of everything i wanted to be, wanted to do. writing code is no longer just a hobby i have, it is me. dont get me wrong i love writing code, its just that i dont really do much outside of it. its just, that interesting to me, and i really enjoy it.

its just that some that point, i think i lost interest in everything else that i could have been - be with friends, video games, sports, getting really good at academics. i guess i was never really good with any of these to begin with. i was never really socially speaking likeable, someone you would talk to. i always wished i could, be better. i guess it started when i learnt how to code, i think it was more than just a hobby to me, the only thing i was, and probably ever could be good at, but i guess that belief brought me further away from what ive wanted to be - approachable, likeable, or really anything that i could have sought enjoyment from being or would have wanted to do.

i wasnt exactly bad at academics either, i was, i think, somewhat mediocre or maybe better than mediocre at it. to be fair i was still not exactly interested in it either, kinematics was the only thing i was even slightly interested in because the applications involved were similar to something else i already knew - programming. i guess i never really could have been better, given everything surrounding my life was programming, that was who i was. i only ever studied because i was forced to, not because i wanted to.

some time later, a few months before my supposed graduation, my dad broke my laptop. the details of why and how are unnecessary, but i guess i couldnt get over it. after all it was all i was, or ever could be good at. at that moment it felt like, everything i knew no longer had any meaning, everything i did, for nothing. it was just, awful i cannot describe the feeling, the awful feeling of wanting to crawl into a hole and die alone, the realization that everything you ever were or ever wanted to be all gone. for days that feel like weeks i just, lay there to waste, rotting alive, not knowing what to do or what to live for. i sometimes wished i had killed myself back then. i think that at some point later, i had, accepted with the fact that i would either die or that for however long i live it wouldnt be the same, that i had to abandon doing what i love or forget what ive done and, well, it sucked. it just sucked.

i guess i was more, fortunate, or arguably unfortunate than i thought. my aunt, the kind woman who gifted me my first laptop, gifted me a second after hearing about it. i dont know if it was for the better or the worse but i accepted it, and i guess my awful routine of, doing nothing but sit on my ass and write code and be online continued, and as much as id hate to admit it i failed high school. i guess it was a consequence of my indecisiveness, or maybe just my sheer stupidity at not being able to handle things.

was i just, not gifted? was i born in the wrong place, in the wrong time, or to the wrong people? to be honest, i wish that were the case. if i was born somewhere better sometime better, and to better people, i probably would have been better off, but i am not. i think i was wrong at what i was doing. maybe i should have listened to things that my parents or other people have said and followed the rigorous path of academics that millions of others have taken and, forced myself to stop doing what i have been doing or who i was. maybe then i could have been, happier. i cant tell anymore. but that didnt happen, and therefore is irrelevant.

sometime after my meaningless activities on code or online in general were reinstated, i met this person. she was, unfortunately, i believe almost exactly who i wanted to be, she had done everything i wish i could and was everything i ever wanted to be, and i believe that she would have done everything that i would have wanted to do, its like the ideal version of myself, the one i dreamt of being for long in my long slumber of meaninglessness. it was just perfect, everytime she shared something from her life i couldnt help but dream how it would have turned it if it were me. i guess i was just a pathetic jealous loser but it brought be joy, and i guess that feeling made me want to observe her from a distance without much interference, i wanted to see how this version of myself i idealized would play out, although sometimes i just felt awful for not being what she was.

until very recently, today even, she brought up to me that she was going to delete all or atleast most of her socials. i just, i dont know what i would be doing for i was, interested in seeing how this ideal version of me would turn out, but at the same time i didnt exactly want to interefere either, for any outside contamination of this ideal version of me, especially from someone pathetic and flawed like myself, could damage it, which i simply would not forgive myself for doing. that news was, to me, not exactly surprising yet brought me that extremely awful feeling once again. she had no reason to be online, she was perfect in what she did and needed no cope on the internet to survive. she had everything she, or rather i would have wanted to have. i guess i can never know because im not her, but boy does it feel that way.

either way, that made me think, what have i done wrong? where have i diverged from being the ideal version of myself? what path could i have taken, a branch i could have avoided? nothing. absolutely nothing i could not fathom a reality where i would have been this ideal version of myself. i have coped reality by being on the internet for far too long, and i dont think i can leave it, and my ideal self now no longer needs it. maybe im just bad at judging whether she is truly happy, or is living her ideal self, maybe she has one who is her ideal version of herself, and she has the same question as i do, but it sure feels like she is living mine and, i guess, is my ideal self.

i do not know what i could have done right, and i dont think i ever will be able to fix, or become my ideal self.

this, just sucks.
